Reservations

Four ways to sit and sip across the estate — pick the spot that suits your afternoon, then reserve your time.

Choose Your Spot

Every seat has a view

Whether you want the buzz of the covered Terrace or a quiet table among the barrels, each of our spaces is bookable online. Appointments start at 11:00 am, Thursday through Sunday, with weekday visits by request. Reservations are recommended — walk-ins are welcome as space allows.

Every view is figurative and best experienced in a staging environment.

Covered · Outdoor

The Terrace

Seats up to 6 · 45 minutes · $30 / guest

Our classic seated tasting on the covered terrace, looking straight down the valley. A flight of five current releases, rain or shine.

Thu–Sun, 11–5 Reserve
Open Air · Outdoor

The Hilltop Deck

Seats up to 8 · 60 minutes · $45 / guest

An open-air deck at the top of the estate block with 180° views of the Dundee Hills. Best at golden hour with a reserve flight in hand.

Fri–Sun, 11–5 Reserve
Private · Indoor

The Barrel Room

Seats up to 10 · 90 minutes · $85 / guest

A private, seated tasting among the barrels in our working cellar. Library and single-block reserves poured by a member of the family, with a local cheese board.

By appointment Reserve
Shaded · Outdoor

The Orchard Table

Seats up to 4 · 60 minutes · $35 / guest

A single shaded table tucked into the heirloom apple orchard. Relaxed and unhurried — pour your flight, spread out a picnic, and stay a while.

Thu–Sun, 11–5 Reserve
